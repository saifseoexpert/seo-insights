# SEO News Roundup: October 13, 2026

**Date:** October 13, 2026  
**Topics:** Spam Update, Fake Authors, AI Content, Helpful Content, AI Overviews, Discover  
**Author:** Saif Ullah, SEO Expert & Digital Marketer

---

## 1. Google's September 2026 spam update is finished

The update began rolling out globally on September 24 and took close to two weeks to complete. Volatility was high during the rollout, and many SEOs reported penalties rising afterwards.

**What to do:** Check your Search Console and analytics for traffic changes between late September and now. If you lost traffic, review thin pages, scaled content, manipulative links and anything that looks auto-generated.

## 2. Google warns against fake author information

Google added guidance to its helpful content documentation warning against deceptive authorship, such as invented authors or fake credentials.

**What to do:** Use real author names, real bios and real expertise. Link author pages to genuine profiles (for example LinkedIn).

## 3. Fact-check your AI-assisted content

Google has told sites to fact-check AI-generated content before publishing it. AI can still help with research and drafts, but a human must verify the claims.

**What to do:** Add a review step to your content process. Check statistics, names, dates and sources before you hit publish.

## 4. Helpful content guidance now stresses "main content"

Google updated its helpful content documentation to put more emphasis on the main content of a page, which is the part that actually serves the visitor.

**What to do:** Put the answer first. Reduce clutter, intrusive ads and filler that pushes the real content down the page.

## 5. AI Overviews are spreading to big-brand searches

AI Overviews are now appearing for many searches involving large brand names, and Google is also testing a "Dive deeper" feature in Discover. Both can answer users without a click.

**What to do:** Create content that offers something AI summaries cannot: original data, first-hand experience and clear expert opinion. Track impressions, not just clicks.

## 6. Search Console performance report recovered

The Search Console performance report was delayed for more than 60 hours over the weekend. It is updating normally again.

**What to do:** Don't judge rankings from delayed data, and keep your own records of key keywords.

---

## Key takeaway

The pattern is consistent: Google rewards **real expertise, original value and trustworthy content**, and it is getting better at filtering out scaled, generic and deceptive pages.

## Today's action checklist

- [ ] Review traffic since the spam update (Sept 24 to now)
- [ ] Audit author bios and credentials
- [ ] Add a fact-checking step to your AI content workflow
- [ ] Make sure the main answer appears at the top of key pages
- [ ] Add original data or examples to your top 5 pages
