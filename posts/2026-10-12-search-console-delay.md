# Google Search Console Delay: Don't Panic, Prepare

**Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Topic:** Google Search Console, Spam Update, Core Update  
**Author:** Saif Ullah, SEO Expert & Digital Marketer

---

## What happened?

Over the weekend of October 10-11, 2026, the Google Search Console **Performance report** ran more than **64 hours behind**, which is very unusual. This report normally updates within a few hours.

The last delay this serious was in **December 2025**.

The delay came right after Google's **September 2026 spam update**, and several third-party SEO tools have also had trouble pulling Google data over the past few weeks.

By October 12, reports are updating normally again.

## Is a core update coming?

Nobody knows for sure. Google has not announced an October 2026 core update, and the delay itself is not proof of one. However, many SEOs expect a core update before the end of the year.

## What I recommend right now

- [ ] **Don't edit pages in a panic.** Reactive changes often hurt more than the update itself.
- [ ] **Don't judge rankings from delayed data.** Wait until reports catch up.
- [ ] **Check technical basics:** crawling, indexing, Core Web Vitals, site speed.
- [ ] **Strengthen content quality:** add original insights, real data and real author details (Google has warned against fake author information).
- [ ] **Document current rankings now,** so you can compare before and after any update.

## Key takeaway

Sites that do well through algorithm changes are not the fastest to react. They are the ones with a **solid foundation**: strong technical SEO, original content and real expertise.
